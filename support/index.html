Jade Gourd

Support

Jade Gourd helps you remember and record your medications, and those of people you look after, on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

Email: app.support@dekideks.com

Say which device and system version you use, and what happened. Please don't send medication details you'd rather keep private; they aren't needed to help.

Common questions

My reminders don't come.

Check Settings in Jade Gourd: Reminders on this device should be on. Then check the iPhone's Settings, Notifications, Jade Gourd. Reminders are off at first on iPad and Mac, so one device can do the reminding.

Jade Gourd sets up reminders ahead of time. If you haven't opened it for a while, a "Keep your reminders coming" notification asks you to open it so the next ones can be set up.

Do reminders come through a Focus or Silent mode?

Reminders are Time Sensitive, so they come through a Focus and aren't held for a Scheduled Summary. You can turn that off in Settings, Notifications, Jade Gourd. They don't ring through Silent mode; set a medication to Alarm for that.

Alarms don't ring.

Your iPhone or iPad asks to allow alarms the first time you save a medication set to Alarm. From version 1.1, Today asks too when a medication is set to Alarm and this device has never asked, as when your medications came from iCloud. If you said no, turn alarms on in Settings, Apps, Jade Gourd; Settings in Jade Gourd shows whether they're allowed. Until then, an alarm medication gets a notification instead. Alarms need iOS or iPadOS 26.1 or later; on a Mac, an alarm medication always gets a notification.

My as-needed medication never reminds me.

That's on purpose. A medication you take only when needed has no schedule, so it has no reminders. Record each dose on Today when you take it. Jade Gourd sets no gap between doses and no daily limit; follow your own instructions.

Does stopping an alarm record the dose?

No. Stopping an alarm only stops the sound. Record the dose as taken or skipped in Jade Gourd, on your iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch.

My other device doesn't show my changes.

Both devices need the same Apple Account with iCloud on for Jade Gourd. Settings in Jade Gourd shows whether sync is on and when it last synced.

Apple Watch or a widget shows old doses.

Apple Watch gets Today from your iPhone, so the iPhone needs to be nearby now and then. A dose you record on the Watch waits there until the iPhone has it. If the Watch says to update Jade Gourd on your iPhone, update it from the App Store. Widgets update through the day, and opening Jade Gourd updates them at once.

I recorded a dose twice.

Each dose shows once, whichever device recorded it. To change an entry, tap it in History.

How do I send someone's medications?

Open the person, then Send a copy. The other phone opens the file in Jade Gourd and chooses whose medications they are. A copy doesn't update: if either of you changes something later, the other's copy stays as it was, so tell them about changes. You'll both get reminders, and what one of you records won't show on the other's phone. Agree who gives each dose.

How do I make a report for a doctor's visit?

Open the person, then Doctor-visit report. Choose the days, then share it as a PDF or print it.

How do I delete everything?

Settings, Delete all data. It erases every person, medication and record; "Me" stays, empty. While iCloud sync is on, it also deletes them from iCloud and from your other devices. Copies you've sent stay where you sent them. To delete one medication or one person, open it and tap Delete.

Does Jade Gourd use Apple Health?

No. It doesn't read or write Health data.

Is this medical advice?

No. Jade Gourd records what you enter. It never calculates or suggests doses. Ask your doctor or pharmacist about your medications.

What does Jade Gourd do with my information?

It stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Jade Gourd Plus

What does Plus add?

Jade Gourd is free for you and one other person, with reminders and alarms, recording, history, iCloud sync, widgets, Apple Watch, the doctor-visit report, export and sending copies. Jade Gourd Plus is one in-app purchase, not a subscription. It adds as many people as you need, and four more looks, each with its own moving light and, on iPhone and iPad, its own app icon. It's in Settings, Jade Gourd Plus.

I don't see Jade Gourd Plus in Settings.

Plus is in version 1.1 and later. If this device hasn't updated yet, update Jade Gourd from the App Store.

I added more than two people before version 1.1.

They all stay, with their reminders and records. From version 1.1, adding anyone else needs Plus.

How do I get Plus on my other devices, or for my family?

Plus comes with your Apple Account, so it's on for every device signed in with it, and Family Sharing gives it to everyone in your family group. If a device doesn't show it, open Settings, Jade Gourd Plus, and tap Restore purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds: ask at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Plus is refunded, nobody is removed and nothing you recorded is lost. Plus's looks and app icons go back to the free ones.
