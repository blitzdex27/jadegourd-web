Jade Gourd

Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

Jade Gourd keeps track of your medications, and the medications of people you look after. It's made by one developer, Dexter Ramos, who doesn't collect, see or sell any of it.

What Jade Gourd stores

  • The people you add, their medications, schedules and reminder settings.
  • What you record for each dose: taken, skipped, the time, a skip reason or a note.
  • Settings for each device, such as whether it reminds you.

Where it's stored

  • On your devices. Everything is kept in the app on your iPhone, iPad or Mac.
  • In your iCloud, if you use iCloud. Jade Gourd syncs through your own private iCloud database, so your devices stay up to date. Apple stores it under your Apple Account; the developer has no access to it. Medication names, strengths, amounts, instructions, notes, skip reasons and people's names are encrypted, so Apple can't read them either. The rest, such as when each dose was due and whether it was taken, is kept there with iCloud's usual protection. With iCloud off for Jade Gourd, nothing goes to iCloud.
  • On your Apple Watch. If you use the Watch app, your iPhone sends it today's and tomorrow's doses. The Watch keeps only the latest list, and any dose you record there until your iPhone has it.
  • In widgets. Widgets on your device show a copy of today's and tomorrow's doses, kept on that device.

What Jade Gourd doesn't do

  • No account, sign-in or server of its own.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking, and no third-party code that collects data.
  • Your information is never sold, and never shared unless you send it yourself.
  • It doesn't use Apple Health (HealthKit).
  • It doesn't calculate or suggest doses, and it isn't medical advice.

When something leaves your devices

Only when you choose. You can send a copy of a person's medications (and, if you choose, their records) as a file, or share a doctor-visit report or an export of your records, through the share sheet: AirDrop, Messages, Mail, Files or another app you pick. What happens to it then depends on where you send it.

Reminders are scheduled on your device. On the Lock Screen, medication names are hidden unless you turn that setting on.

How long it's kept

Your records stay until you delete them. Jade Gourd doesn't delete old records on its own.

Deleting your information

  • A medication or a person: open it and tap Delete. It goes with its records, from this device and, while iCloud sync is on, from iCloud and your other devices.
  • Delete all data in Jade Gourd's Settings erases every person, medication, schedule and record. "Me" stays, empty. While iCloud sync is on, they're also deleted from iCloud, and so from your other devices. Each device's settings stay, and copies you've sent stay where you sent them.
  • Deleting the app removes what's on that device. The iCloud copy stays until you use Delete all data, or delete Jade Gourd's data in your iCloud storage settings.

Children

Jade Gourd isn't made for children to use on their own. A parent or carer can add a child as a person they look after; that information stays where described above.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date, and big changes are mentioned in the app's release notes.
