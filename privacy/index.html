Jade Gourd

Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

Jade Gourd keeps track of your medications, and the medications of people you look after. It's made by one developer, Dexter Ramos, who doesn't collect, see or sell any of it.

What Jade Gourd stores

  • The people you add, their medications, schedules and reminder settings.
  • What you record for each dose: taken, skipped, the time, a skip reason or a note.
  • Settings for each device, such as whether it reminds you.

Where it's stored

  • On your devices. Everything is kept in the app on your iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Vision Pro or Apple Watch.
  • In your iCloud, if you use iCloud. Jade Gourd syncs through your own private iCloud database, so your devices stay up to date. Apple stores it under your Apple Account; the developer has no access to it. Medication names, strengths, instructions, notes, skip reasons and people's names are encrypted, so Apple can't read them either. With iCloud off for Jade Gourd, nothing leaves the device.
  • On your Apple Watch. If you use the Watch app, your iPhone sends it today's doses.
  • In widgets. Widgets on your device show a copy of today's doses, kept on that device.

What Jade Gourd doesn't do

  • No account, sign-in or server of its own.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking, and no third-party code that collects data.
  • Your information is never sold or shared.
  • It doesn't use Apple Health (HealthKit).
  • It doesn't calculate or suggest doses, and it isn't medical advice.

When something leaves your devices

Only when you choose. You can share a person's medications (and, if you choose, their records) as a file, or share a doctor-visit report or an export of your records, through the share sheet: AirDrop, Messages, Mail, Files or another app you pick. What happens to it then depends on where you send it.

Reminders are scheduled on your device. On the Lock Screen, medication names are hidden unless you turn that setting on.

Deleting your information

  • Delete all data in Jade Gourd's Settings removes everything from this device and from your iCloud, and so from your other devices.
  • Deleting the app removes what's on that device. The iCloud copy stays until you use Delete all data, or remove it in your device's iCloud settings (Apple Account, iCloud, Manage Storage).

Children

Jade Gourd isn't made for children to use on their own. A parent or carer can add a child as a person they look after; that information stays where described above.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date, and big changes are mentioned in the app's release notes.

Questions about privacy: app.support@dekideks.com
